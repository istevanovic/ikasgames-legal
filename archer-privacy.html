Archer's Range Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Archer's Range is published by Ilija Stevanovic. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

Information the game stores

The game stores gameplay preferences, scores, unlocked content, and purchase-entitlement status on your device. This information is used to provide game progress and features.

Advertising and tracking

The game may display ads provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its partners may process device information, IP address, ad performance data, and, where you grant permission, the advertising identifier (IDFA), to deliver and measure ads. You can decline tracking permission in iOS Settings. See Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment-card details. Apple may process information according to its own privacy policy.

Support

If you email us for support, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and issue description. We use it only to respond to your request.

Children's privacy

Archer's Range is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support email, contact us so we can address the request.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Ilija Stevanovic at ikas92@gmail.com.
