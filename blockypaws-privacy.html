Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

ikasgames (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates Blocky Paws Blast (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the App.

Information stored on your device

The App does not require an account and does not ask you to submit your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or other personal information. Game progress such as high scores, preferences, and ad-removal status may be stored locally on your device using Apple’s standard storage technologies.

Advertising and tracking

The App uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertising. Depending on your device settings, consent choices, and applicable law, Google and its service providers may process information such as an advertising or device identifier, IP address, general location inferred from IP address, ad interactions, app interactions, performance information, and crash or diagnostic information. Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework may ask for permission before information is used to track you across apps and websites.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Purchases

The App may offer an optional ad-removal purchase through Apple’s App Store. Payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple may process purchase and transaction information under its own privacy policy.

How we use information

Information processed by the App or its service providers is used to operate the App, show and measure advertising, maintain security, diagnose problems, process purchases, and improve performance.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support advertising, analytics related to advertising, App Store purchases, and technical operation of the App, as described above.

Children

The App is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Your choices

You can manage tracking permission in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can reset locally stored App data by deleting the App, subject to Apple’s device and backup behavior. You can also contact us with questions or privacy requests.

Data retention and security

Local game data remains on your device until it is deleted or cleared. Data processed by third-party providers is retained according to their policies. We use reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or its data practices change. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
