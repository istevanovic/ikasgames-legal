Blocky Paws Blast Support

Need help with Blocky Paws Blast? We’re happy to help with gameplay, purchases, ads, bugs, and accessibility questions.

Contact us

Email ikas92@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem, please include your device model, iOS version, App version, and a short description of what happened. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Common help

  • Purchase restoration: Open the in-app Remove Ads screen and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.
  • Ads and tracking: Tracking permission can be managed in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Lost local scores: Scores and preferences are stored locally on the device and may be removed when the App is deleted or device data is cleared.
